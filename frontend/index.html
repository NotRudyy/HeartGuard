    About HeartGuard

    HeartGuard estimates heart attack risk from age, gender, blood pressure, cholesterol, resting heart rate, smoking, diabetes, family history and symptoms. Each factor adds points, and the total becomes a score out of 10 and a Low, Moderate or High level.

    It is a college project for education. It is not a diagnosis. Always speak to a doctor about your heart health. In an emergency call 108 or 112.
